EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — 2026 EDITION

Welcome to the company! This handbook covers essential policies and procedures.

WORK HOURS AND FLEXIBILITY

Core hours: 10:00 AM — 3:00 PM (local time)
Flexible window: 7:00 AM — 7:00 PM
Remote work: Up to 3 days per week with manager approval
Hybrid schedule: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday in-office recommended

PTO POLICY

- New employees: 15 days PTO per year
- After 3 years: 20 days PTO per year
- After 7 years: 25 days PTO per year
- Sick leave: 10 days per year (separate from PTO)
- Parental leave: 16 weeks paid (birthing parent), 8 weeks paid (non-birthing)

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days. Receipts required for all purchases over $25. Approved categories:
- Travel (flights, hotels, ground transportation)
- Meals during business travel (up to $75/day)
- Professional development (courses, conferences, books)
- Home office equipment (up to $500 one-time)

IT SECURITY POLICY

1. All devices must use full-disk encryption
2. VPN required for remote access to internal systems
3. Passwords: minimum 14 characters, rotated every 90 days
4. Multi-factor authentication required for all SaaS applications
5. Report security incidents to security@company.com within 1 hour

ETHICS AND CONDUCT

We maintain zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, and fraud. All employees must complete annual ethics training. Concerns can be reported through the anonymous hotline: 1-800-555-ETHC.

EMERGENCY CONTACTS

HR Department: hr@company.com
Facilities: facilities@company.com
Security: security@company.com
